  Literature Searching Assignment: Developing a Multi-Database Search Strategy
                                    Based on PICOS

1. Assignment Title

The Effectiveness of Artificial Intelligence–Powered Conversational Agents in Supporting
Self-Management and Improving Glycemic Control Among Adults With Type 2 Diabetes:
A Multi-Database Search for Randomized Controlled Trials

2. Background

Type 2 diabetes is a chronic condition that requires continuous self-management,
including blood glucose monitoring, medication use, dietary management, physical
activity, problem-solving, and communication with healthcare professionals. Nurses play
an important role in diabetes education, behavioral support, follow-up, and care
coordination.

Artificial intelligence–powered conversational agents—including chatbots, virtual health
assistants, embodied conversational agents, and voice assistants—can provide
automated education, personalized feedback, reminders, behavioral support, and
medication-management assistance. These technologies may supplement nurse-led
diabetes management by delivering continuous support outside clinical settings.

Randomized studies have evaluated conversational agents for diabetes self-management
and voice-based AI systems for insulin management. However, the interventions,
outcomes, and terminology used in this field vary considerably, making a structured and
reproducible literature-search strategy necessary.

3. Aim of the Assignment

The purpose of this assignment is to develop, conduct, document, and critically evaluate
a reproducible multi-database literature search concerning AI-powered conversational
interventions for adults with type 2 diabetes.

Students are expected to:

   1. Translate a clinical nursing question into a PICOS framework. [5 marks]
